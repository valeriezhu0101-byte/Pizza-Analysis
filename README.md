# Pizza Sales Analysis 
**Interactive Dashboard Creation Using MS Excel and Data Visualization Using Python**
## Project Objective
The Pizza Sales Analysis project explores transactional sales data from a pizza store to identify key business insights, sales trends, and key performance indicators (KPIs).

Using **Microsoft Excel** and **Python**, the project analyzes customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and sales trends to support data-driven decision-making in sales, marketing, and business operations.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/valeriezhu0101-byte/Pizza-Analysis/blob/main/pizza_sales.csv">Pizza Sales Dataset</a>

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Revenue:** The sum of the total price of all pizza orders.
- **Total Pizzas Sold:** The total quantity of pizzas sold.
- **Total Orders:** The total number of orders placed.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** The average revenue generated per order, calculated by dividing total revenue by total orders.
- **Average Pizzas per Order:** The average number of pizzas sold per order, calculated by dividing total pizzas sold by total orders.

## Analysis & Visualizations
### Ingredient Analysis
Analyzes the frequency of ingredient usage across different pizza varieties to identify the most commonly used ingredients.
- Identify high-demand ingredients to optimize inventory management and reduce food waste.
- Support purchasing decisions and ensure sufficient stock of frequently used ingredients.

### Daily Trend Analysis
A bar chart visualizing total orders, pizzas sold, and revenue by day of the week (Monday to Sunday).
- Compare sales performance across weekdays and weekends.
- Identify the busiest days to support weekly operational planning.

### Hourly Trend Analysis
Visualizes total orders, pizzas sold, and revenue by hour of the day to understand customer purchasing patterns throughout the day.
- Identify peak ordering hours and periods of low demand.
- Optimize staff scheduling and inventory preparation based on hourly demand.

### Monthly Trend for Total Orders
A line chart showing the total number of orders by month to track changes in customer demand over time.
- Helps track seasonality and identify peak sales months.
- Summer months show higher sales, potentially driven by promotional campaigns.

### Monthly Trend for Total Pizzas
A line chart depicting monthly revenue and orders.
- Helps track seasonality and identify peak sales months.
- Summer months show higher sales due to promotional campaigns.

### % of Sales by Pizza Category
A donut chart representing distribution of sales across different pizza categories (Classic, Supreme, Veggie, Chicken).
- Helps identify customer preferences.
- Classic pizzas dominate sales, while Veggie has lower demand.

### % of Sales by Pizza Size
A pie chart representing the distribution of sales across different pizza sizes (S, M, L, XL, XXL).
- Helps identify customer preferences.
- Large (L) pizzas contribute the highest revenue.

### % Sales by Pizza Size & Category
A bar/ donut chart comparing sales revenue and quantity by pizza size (S, M, L, XL, XXL).
- Highlights demand distribution by size and assist inventory planning.

### Total Pizzas Sold by Category
A bar/funnel chart comparing the total quantity of pizzas sold across different pizza categories.
- Manage inventory by stocking ingredients used in the most popular categories.
- Evaluate if low-performing categories should be optimized, redesigned, or discontinued.

### Top 5 Best-Selling Pizzas
Bar charts highlighting the top 5 best-selling pizzas based on total revenue, number of orders, or quantity sold.
- Identify the most popular and highest-performing pizza varieties.
- Support menu optimization and promotional strategies.

### Bottom 5 Worst-Selling Pizzas
Bar charts highlighting the 5 lowest-performing pizzas based on total revenue, number of orders, or quantity sold.
- Identify underperforming pizza varieties.
- Support menu optimization and decisions on pricing, promotions, or product discontinuation.

## Business Questions Answered
- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many pizzas were sold in total?
- Which category and size of pizzas perform best?
- Which pizzas are the top and bottom performers?
- What is the average order value and average pizzas per order?
